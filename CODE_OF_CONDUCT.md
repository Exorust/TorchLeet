# Code of Conduct

TorchLeet is a study resource used mostly by people preparing for interviews,
which is a stressful thing to be doing. Be kind to them.

- Assume good faith. "This is wrong" is fine; "you are an idiot" is not.
- Do not post proprietary interview material, or anything covered by an NDA.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments. Nothing about someone's
  background, identity, or level of experience.
- Keep discussion on the problem.

Report problems to chandrahas.aroori@gmail.com. Maintainers may edit, remove, or
block for behaviour that breaks these rules.
